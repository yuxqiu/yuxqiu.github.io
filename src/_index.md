Hi! I'm a first-year Ph.D. student in Computer and Information Science at the University of Pennsylvania and a member of the [Schlein Center for Cybersecurity](https://cybersecurity.engineering.upenn.edu/), advised by [Sebastian Angel](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~sga001/).

I'm interested in making computation provably secure and verifiable while keeping it practical. More concretely, I work on proof systems and on building and deploying them efficiently, drawing on ideas from programming languages, automated reasoning, and hardware acceleration.

Before coming to Penn, I got a master's degree at University College London, where I was advised by [Philipp Jovanovic](https://philippjovanovic.com/) and [Alberto Sonnino](https://albertosonnino.com/). I also spent a year as a B.S. CS exchange student at Georgia Tech.
